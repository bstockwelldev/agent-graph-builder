Sticky notes hold reference text on the canvas: why a step exists, what to check, or a question for whoever edits next. Each note shows its author and has its own comment thread. Notes are saved with the graph but are for people only: they never change what runs, a release's diff, or the graph's fingerprint.

## How do I…
- **Add a note:** right-click the empty canvas ▸ **Add sticky note**, right-click a node ▸ **Add note** (pinned to that node), or ⌘K ▸ **Add sticky note**.
- **Write and style it:** select the note; edit its text and color in the inspector. Drag it anywhere.
- **Pin it to a node:** choose the node under **Pinned to**. A pinned note moves with its node. If the node is deleted, the note stays, unpinned.
- **Comment:** type under **Add a comment** and press **Comment** (⌘↵). Mark the note **Resolved** when the discussion is done.
- **Sign your notes:** when you're signed in, your email is used; otherwise set **Your name** once and it's remembered in this browser.
- **Delete:** select the note and press Delete, or use the trash button. Undo brings it back.
- **See them all:** the status bar's **Notes** button (or ⌘K ▸ **Show all notes**) opens the Notes panel: filter Open, Resolved or All, and click one to jump to it.
- **Hide them:** turn off **Show notes on canvas** in the Notes panel (or ⌘K). They stay saved and listed.
