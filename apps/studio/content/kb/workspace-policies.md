Workspace policies sets the default enforcement for each rule (security, reliability, cost, governance) that every graph inherits. It also lists the time-boxed exceptions granted on any graph, so waivers that are about to lapse get noticed.

## How do I…
- **Change a default:** pick Off, Warn or Block on a rule. It saves at once.
- **See or edit one graph's overrides:** use the Scope picker, or open that graph's own **Policies** panel.
- **Extend or revoke an exception:** use the buttons on its row.
