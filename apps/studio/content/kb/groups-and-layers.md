- **Groups** frame related nodes. Select nodes and choose **Group**; collapse a group to tidy the canvas.
- **Layers** tag nodes by concern. The header's **View** menu shows them as lanes, and **Manage layers** edits them.
- **Heatmap** colors nodes by run metrics p95 latency, failure rate or executions.
- **Extract to subgraph** moves a selection into its own graph and replaces it with a [Subgraph node](kb:node-subgraph).
