Replay re-runs a past run from its recorded graph snapshot. A **frozen** replay reproduces it; a **counterfactual** replay changes one thing, such as forcing a router down a different edge or swapping an LLM node's provider or model, so you can compare the outcome.

## How do I…
- Open the run from the Run panel's history and choose **Replay**.
