Policies are rules every graph is checked against, such as "LLM models must be pinned". Each policy is an error or a warning, and can block publishing.

## How do I…
- **Set workspace defaults:** the **Policies** page.
- **Override for one graph:** the graph's **Policies** panel (⋯ menu).
- **Waive one temporarily:** add an exception with a reason and an expiry date; it lapses on its own.
